HUM AND CHORD FOR MAC

INSTALL
1. Open Terminal (Applications > Utilities > Terminal).
2. Type  bash  and a space, drag Install.command from this folder into the
   Terminal window, and press Return.
   (Running it this way avoids macOS blocking a script from the internet.)
3. Quit and reopen Logic and Reaper.

LOGIC
- On a software instrument track, click the MIDI FX slot (above the
  instrument) and choose Audio Units > Hum and Chord > Hum and Chord.
- Press play. Listen mode shows the key and a chord for every bar.
- With Logic stopped, play your keyboard and Play mode suggests what to
  play next.
- Chords you click play through the track's instrument. Record-enable the
  track to record them.
- If it doesn't appear: Logic Pro > Settings > Plug-in Manager, find
  Hum and Chord, and click "Reset & Rescan Selection".

REAPER
- Options > Preferences > Plug-ins > VST, click "Re-scan".
- On a track, add "VST3i: Hum and Chord" to the FX chain, ABOVE your
  instrument, so the instrument plays the chords you click.

YOUR SONGS
- Songs you build in the plugin are saved inside your Logic or Reaper
  project.
- Singing needs the microphone, which plugins can't use. Use the browser
  version (hum-and-chord.html in Chrome) or the Hum and Chord app for that.
