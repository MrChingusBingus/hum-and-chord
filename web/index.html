Notes from this track go to Listen while your DAW is playing, and to Play when it's stopped, so you can try chords on your keyboard.

Help

Using the plugin

Logic: put Hum and Chord in the track's MIDI FX slot, above the instrument. Chords you click play through that instrument.

Logic audio tracks: put "Hum and Chord Audio" in an Audio FX slot. It listens to what's recorded, or to you live if the track is record-enabled with input monitoring on.

Reaper: add the Hum and Chord VST3 to any track. On a MIDI track, put it before your instrument.

Singing: use it on an audio track with your mic, turn on input monitoring, and press Start listening in Sing.

Your songs are saved inside your DAW project.

Connect your DAW

Mac:

  1. Open Audio MIDI Setup, choose Window → Show MIDI Studio, double-click IAC Driver and tick "Device is online".
  2. In your DAW, send the track's MIDI out to "IAC Driver Bus 1".
  3. Here, press Connect and mark IAC Driver as DAW in Settings.
  4. To follow bars and tempo, turn on "Send MIDI clock" to IAC in your DAW's sync settings.

Windows: install the free loopMIDI app, create a port, and use it the same way.

No setup: export the clip from your DAW as a MIDI file and use Open MIDI file in Listen.

If you also send chords back to your DAW, use a second port for that, so the app doesn't hear its own chords.

Keyboard

Plug it in with USB and use Chrome or Edge. Safari can't read MIDI devices.

Singing

Sing one note at a time, close to the mic. Headphones stop the app from hearing its own sound.

Chord names

"C" is C major (happy), "Cm" is C minor (sad). Numbers like 7 add extra color. Roman numerals (I, IV, vi) show where a chord sits in the key.
